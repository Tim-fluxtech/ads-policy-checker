FAQ

Questions, answered

Does a "Low risk" result mean Meta will approve my ad?

No. It means the copy, targeting and page don't match any of the risks we check for. Meta makes the final decision, also looks at images and video, and changes its rules. Treat the result as a pre-flight check.

Can you help me advertise a prohibited product?

No. For products Meta doesn't allow at all (vapes, payday loans, THC, adult services and similar) we explain why and stop. Disguising them with misspellings or cloaked pages is the fastest way to lose a whole Business Manager.

Where do the rules come from?

From Meta's public Advertising Standards and Business Help Centre pages. Each issue links to the page it's based on, and we show the date the rules were last checked.

Can you get my Business Manager or ad account back?

Nobody outside Meta can honestly promise that. What we do is work out exactly what's restricted, show you Meta's official route for your case, help you prepare the right documents and a factual appeal, and track deadlines. Pro members can chat with a rep, and we offer calls for Business Manager, ad account, hacked-admin and recommendation issues.

Should I just open a new ad account?

No. Creating new or rented accounts to get around a restriction is treated as ban evasion and usually makes the restriction permanent, often for every account linked to you. We'll never suggest it.

How does the Guardian Growth agency service work?

We run your Facebook ads for you: strategy, creatives, launch, testing, scaling and reporting. We start with a free profit audit to find your break-even cost per sale, then manage your campaigns to it. You keep ownership of your ad account and give us partner access through your Business portfolio.

Do you guarantee profit or ROAS?

No. Anyone who guarantees returns on ads is guessing. We show you the maths before you spend, cut what doesn't work quickly, and report what you actually kept.

Do you need my Meta login?

Never. You paste your ad here and act inside Meta yourself. Nothing is sent to Meta.

Do the rewrites invent claims?

No. Example features show in [brackets] until you add your real details, and social-proof templates use placeholders for real numbers. AI rewrites are told not to invent facts and are checked by the same rules.
